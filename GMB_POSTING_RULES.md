# Google Business Profile Posting Rules

Use this file as the required checklist for every future Google Business Profile (GBP/GMB) post created by this project.

## 1. Goal

Create useful, accurate, city-relevant posts that help customers understand the service and take action. Posts should support local relevance and conversions without keyword stuffing, false claims, duplicate content, or misleading location signals.

Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence. Posts can strengthen profile quality and customer engagement, but posting frequency or keyword repetition cannot overcome distance or guarantee rankings.

Official guidance:

- Local ranking: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091
- GBP posts: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169
- Photos and videos: https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862

## 2. Required inputs

Confirm these fields before creating a post:

- Target profile ID and business name
- Target city and 2-4 genuine neighbourhoods
- Primary service
- Specific customer problem, project type, or educational angle
- Correct city landing page
- CTA type, normally `LEARN_MORE`
- Image source: real project photo, approved supplied image, or disclosed AI-generated creative
- Whether the request is a draft, scheduled post, or immediate live post

Never guess a profile when multiple profiles could match. Verify the profile and city mapping first. A service-area city may use the closest legitimate EPF hub only when that mapping is accurate.

## 3. Profile and city targeting

- Use a dedicated city profile when one exists and genuinely represents that location.
- Use an EPF service hub for a nearby service-area city only when the business actually serves that city.
- Do not imply that EPF has a storefront or office in a city where it only provides mobile services.
- Do not add fake addresses, landmarks, coordinates, project claims, or neighbourhood coverage.
- Distance is a Google-controlled ranking factor; city wording does not make a distant profile local.
- Re-check live profile data before publishing because names, links, defaults, and status can change.

## 4. City landing links

Use the exact matching EPF city page whenever available:

- Oakville: https://epfproservices.com/popcorn-ceiling-removal/oakville/
- Burlington: https://epfproservices.com/popcorn-ceiling-removal/burlington/
- Milton: https://epfproservices.com/popcorn-ceiling-removal/milton/
- Mississauga: https://epfproservices.com/popcorn-ceiling-removal/mississauga/
- Hamilton: https://epfproservices.com/popcorn-ceiling-removal/hamilton/

For another city, locate and verify the official page before posting. Never invent a URL. Use the city page for the CTA instead of the homepage when a relevant city page exists.

Add campaign tracking when reporting supports it:

```text
?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp_<city>_<service>
```

Use lowercase words separated by underscores and avoid creating a different naming convention for every post.

## 5. Caption structure

Write approximately 100-220 words unless the post type requires something shorter.

1. Opening: service + city or one neighbourhood in a natural sentence.
2. Local context: mention 2-4 genuine neighbourhoods, building types, or local project conditions.
3. Problem: explain one customer need such as painted texture, cracks, patches, high ceilings, condo logistics, or dust control.
4. Process: describe relevant work such as protection, removal or encapsulation, repairs, skim coating, HEPA sanding, primer, and optional painting.
5. Result: state a realistic benefit without guarantees or exaggeration.
6. CTA: invite the reader to request an estimate and use the matching city landing page.
7. Hashtags: use 3-5 relevant hashtags at most.

Suggested template:

```text
Planning [SERVICE] in [CITY]?

EPF Pro Services helps homeowners in [NEIGHBOURHOODS] with [SPECIFIC PROBLEM OR PROJECT TYPE].

Our process includes [RELEVANT PROCESS STEPS]. The goal is [REALISTIC RESULT] with careful preparation, clear communication, and tidy cleanup.

View our [CITY] service and request an estimate:
[CITY LANDING PAGE]

#[Service] #[City] #[UsefulLocalTag]
```

## 6. Local-signal rules

Good local signals:

- The correct city once in the opening
- Two to four real neighbourhoods used naturally
- Genuine housing or project context, such as a condo, detached home, townhome, high foyer, or open main floor
- A city-specific service page
- Accurate service-area and business information
- Real project details when supplied by the customer or project record

Do not use:

- Long lists of cities or neighbourhoods
- Repeated city-service phrases
- Keywords for a different city, such as Hamilton terms in a Mississauga post
- Claims that an AI image is a real completed EPF project
- Fake project dates, customer stories, reviews, awards, warranties, prices, or guarantees
- Phone numbers in the post body when the CTA/profile already supplies contact details; Google notes that posts containing phone numbers may be rejected
- Hashtag blocks or awkward exact-match keyword combinations

## 7. Uniqueness and cadence

- Every caption must be meaningfully different from recent posts on the same profile.
- Check at least the latest 8 posts before drafting.
- Do not reuse the same opening, paragraph structure, neighbourhood list, CTA sentence, image, or hashtag set consecutively.
- Rotate content angles: completed result, before/after, process, FAQ, painted texture, crack repair, dust control, condo logistics, high ceilings, customer review, seasonal scheduling, or offer.
- Avoid publishing several similar posts to one profile within minutes unless the user explicitly requests it and understands the posts may compete with each other.
- Prefer a consistent quality schedule over bulk publication. Treat cadence as a content-management choice, not a guaranteed ranking factor.

## 8. Image rules

Prefer authentic project evidence:

- Real before-and-after photos
- Protected work areas
- Scraping, repairs, skim coating, sanding, priming, or painting
- Finished smooth ceilings
- Crew and equipment where appropriate
- Short real project videos

Google recommends focused, well-lit JPG or PNG images that represent reality and are not excessively altered or filtered.

For AI-generated promotional creatives:

- Use them as marketing illustrations, not proof of a completed project.
- Do not say or imply that the pictured room belongs to an EPF customer.
- Mix AI creatives with genuine project photos; do not let AI graphics replace the real portfolio.
- Use a realistic residential interior and make the ceiling the main subject.
- Recommended working format: landscape 4:3, JPG or PNG, under 5 MB.
- Keep important text inside generous safe margins.
- Use only exact, approved image text.
- Limit image text to service, city, brand, and one short value line.
- Check spelling, room geometry, lighting, branding, and legibility before use.
- Avoid fake badges, star ratings, reviews, prices, phone numbers, guarantees, watermarks, or competitor branding.

Recommended image text:

```text
POPCORN CEILING REMOVAL
[CITY]
EPF PRO SERVICES
Clean. Smooth. Professional.
```

## 9. Reviews and prominence

Posts are only one part of local presence. Maintain these supporting signals:

- Complete and accurate profile categories, services, hours, phone, website, and service areas
- Genuine customer reviews acquired without incentives or review gating
- Helpful owner responses written for the actual review
- Fresh real photos and videos
- Consistent business information on EPF's website and trusted directories
- Strong city pages with unique local content and internal links
- Relevant local mentions and backlinks

Never fabricate reviews or insert city/service keywords into a response when they do not match the customer's experience.

## 10. CTA and tracking

- Use `LEARN_MORE` with the matching city service page for educational and service posts.
- Use an offer post only when the terms, dates, eligibility, and price are real and approved.
- Keep one primary action per post.
- Track the post ID, profile ID, city, service, landing URL, media URL, publish time, status, and Google post URL.
- Review GBP performance for website clicks, calls, messages, and search visibility; do not judge success only by post count.

## 11. Pre-publication checklist

Before any live post, confirm all items:

- [ ] Correct business profile and location ID
- [ ] Correct city and legitimate service area
- [ ] Correct, working EPF city URL
- [ ] Unique caption checked against recent history
- [ ] Service and city appear naturally
- [ ] Only 2-4 genuine neighbourhoods
- [ ] No unrelated city keywords
- [ ] No false, unverifiable, or guaranteed claims
- [ ] No unnecessary phone number in the description
- [ ] CTA and link match the caption
- [ ] Image text is spelled correctly
- [ ] Image is relevant, legible, realistic, and within Google's format/size guidance
- [ ] AI image is not presented as a real customer project
- [ ] Explicit user approval received for the exact live post, unless an existing approved automation rule applies

## 12. Post-publication verification

After publishing:

1. Capture the returned Google post ID and URL.
2. Confirm the backend history records `POSTED`.
3. If Google returns `PROCESSING`, report that status accurately and re-check when required.
4. Confirm the CTA opens the correct city landing page.
5. Confirm the expected image is attached.
6. Record or report any rejection, stripped CTA, missing image, or incorrect profile immediately.

## 13. Quality standard

The final post should sound like a useful local contractor update written for homeowners, not an SEO keyword container. Accuracy, real evidence, clear service information, and a relevant city landing page take priority over keyword volume.
